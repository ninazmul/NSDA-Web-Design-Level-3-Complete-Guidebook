About This Guidebook

What is the NSDA Web Design Level 3?

A competency-based qualification under the Bangladesh National Qualification Framework (BNQF) developed by the National Skills Development Authority.

📘 Competency Standard

A competency standard is a written specification of the knowledge, skills, and attitudes required for the performance of an occupation, trade, or job corresponding to industry standards.

🎯 Purpose

Training & Assessment: Provides a consistent framework for training programs and assessment tools.

Industry Recognition: Enables industry-recognized qualifications through direct assessment of workplace competencies.

Flexible Learning: Encourages development and delivery of flexible training suited to individual and industry requirements.

🏢 BNQF Level 3 — Skilled Worker

Knowledge: Moderately broad knowledge in a specific work or study area, able to perceive ideas and abstract from drawing and design.

Skills: Basic cognitive and practical skills required to use relevant information, carry out tasks, and solve routine problems using simple rules and tools.

Responsibility: Work or study under supervision with considerable autonomy. Participate in teams and responsible for group coordination.
